Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DuoDuo ("the app," "we," "us," or "our"), listed on the App Store as DuoDuo: Folding Book, a folding-book puzzle game, handles information when you use it.

Short version

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The app does not collect personal data and does not send any data to us. We don't operate any servers for it.
  • There is no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, DuoDuo stores a small amount of data locally on your device:

  • which levels you have completed
  • whether the full book is unlocked
  • whether you prefer the fold dial over the hinge

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app deletes it. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), which are governed by your platform provider.

Hinge and motion

On foldable devices DuoDuo reads the hinge angle and fold position from your device so the game can fold with it. These readings are used immediately on your device to draw the game and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The full-book purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether the purchase is owned on your account.

Third-party platforms

DuoDuo relies on operating system and platform features such as local storage, hinge sensors, haptics, and in-app purchases. Those platform providers, such as Apple and Google, may handle limited technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children

DuoDuo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through a support request. See the support page for other ways to reach us.
