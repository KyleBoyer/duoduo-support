Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DuoDuo ("DuoDuo," "the app," "we," "us," or "our"), listed on the App Store and Google Play as DuoDuo: Folding Puzzle, handles information when you play it.

Short version

  • DuoDuo does not collect any personal data.
  • No account registration is required.
  • The app doesn't send anything to us. We don't operate any servers for it.
  • There are no ads, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics in the app.

Information stored on your device

DuoDuo saves your game progress on your device: which levels you've finished, how many hints you have, and your settings (such as wanderer mode and whether to use the on-screen fold). This information never leaves your device except as part of backups you make with your phone's own backup features.

Folding hinge

On foldable phones, DuoDuo reads the angle of the phone's hinge so that folding the phone folds the puzzle. The angle is used only while you play and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The full game and hint packs are sold through the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock it.

Third-party platforms

DuoDuo relies on operating system features such as local storage, haptics, and in-app purchases. Those platform providers, such as Apple and Google, may handle limited technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children

DuoDuo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through a support request. See the support page for other ways to reach us.
