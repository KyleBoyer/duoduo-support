DuoDuo Support

Help for DuoDuo (listed on the App Store as DuoDuo: Folding Book) on iPhone and foldable Android phones.

Get help

The fastest way to get help, report a bug, or suggest a feature is to open a GitHub issue. Requests are usually answered within a few days.

Open a support request Browse existing issues

Please don't post personal details. GitHub issues are public.

Helpful things to include:

  • Your device and OS version (for example, iPhone Duo, iOS 27.2)
  • The app version (shown in the App Store or Google Play listing)
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Frequently asked questions

How do I fold the book?

On a foldable phone, fold the phone itself: the real hinge folds the world. On other phones, drag, pinch or use the fold dial at the side of the screen. You can switch a foldable to the dial under the menu with Fold with the dial instead of the hinge.

The hinge isn't doing anything

The hinge only drives the game while the phone is open and the fold runs through the game screen. With the phone shut, on the cover display, or in split screen the game falls back to the dial. If the game still ignores the hinge, close and reopen the app.

How do I unlock the full book?

The first two chapters, Pop-Up and Crease, are free. One purchase unlocks Chomp, Squeezebox and Cover, plus every chapter added later. There are no ads and no subscriptions. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

I reinstalled and lost my unlock

Open the menu and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same App Store or Google account you bought with.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Can't use GitHub?

If you can't open an issue, you can email the developer instead. GitHub issues get a faster reply.
