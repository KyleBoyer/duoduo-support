DuoDuo Support

Help for DuoDuo: Folding Puzzle on iPhone and Android.

Get help

The fastest way to get help, report a bug, or suggest a feature is to open a GitHub issue. Requests are usually answered within a few days.

Open a support request Browse existing issues

GitHub issues are public. Please don't include personal details.

Helpful things to include:

  • Your device and OS version (for example, iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1)
  • The chapter and level (shown at the top of the screen, like "Crease · 2")
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Frequently asked questions

How do I fold the puzzle?

On a foldable phone, just fold the phone: the real hinge folds the puzzle. On other phones, drag up or down on the puzzle, pinch, or use the fold dial on the right edge.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap the light bulb for the level's clue. Hints show your next move, and a walkthrough plays the whole level for you. You start with free hints, and more are available as a purchase.

If tiles are hard to tap when the fold is nearly closed, turn on wanderer mode (the footprints button): tap near a dot and your wanderer walks there.

How do snaps and pumps work?

In Chomp, snap the fold shut quickly and open it again to move the pink flaps. In Squeezebox, close the fold a little and open it again, briskly, to pump air into the bags. Both chapters also have a button at the bottom of the screen that does it for you.

I bought the full game but chapters are still locked

Open the menu, choose a locked chapter, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no server. Your progress is saved on your device.

How do I reset my progress?

Deleting the app removes your saved progress. Purchases stay with your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored after reinstalling.

Can't use GitHub?

If you can't open an issue, you can email the developer instead. GitHub issues get a faster reply.
